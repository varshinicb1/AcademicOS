## Traps

- `test_primary_books.py` and any other test that plans from the real date can go red as the year moves on.
  Freeze `curriculum.routes._school_today` in such tests.
- The full backend suite takes about 12 minutes on 4 cores. Do not run two at once on one machine.
- About 60 stale branches remain on the AcademicOS remote, most from before September's history rewrite.
  Their work is merged or superseded; deleting them is the owner's call.
