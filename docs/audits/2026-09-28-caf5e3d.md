## Defects

New, numbered from D74. The earlier ones are in `2026-09-27-8db6636.md`.

- **D74 (exposure regression).** The Section E slots Task 125 now fills print CBE questions that cannot be answered, where those slots used to be empty:
  - Maths 6: `Maths6KK5`, "512512 of a wall white and 1313...", doubled fractions.
  - Maths 9: `Maths9AG3`, "(𝑥+ 𝑥 ) = Rs. 7", with the 1/x lost.
  - Maths 10: `Maths10AD8`, "tabulated above", with no table.
  - Science 7: `SCIENCE7ARP`, which needs a missing figure.

  That is 3 of 13 distinct Section E questions unanswerable, plus 1 that needs a figure. *Repro:* generate the half-yearly or annual preset for each pair locally on the served bank.
- **D75.** "Case based" Section E accepts any 4-mark CBE question, not only case studies. Example: `Maths9AG4`, "Draw the graph of 3x−2=0...", whose key ends with the next item's id.
- **D76 (pre-existing, one more placement).** CBE stems print "(Total N marks)" that contradicts the printed marks. It went from 13 to 14 placements; `Science9TP5` now prints "(Total 12 marks)" in a 4-mark slot.
- **D77 (pre-existing questions, newly printed).** Truncated or wrong-part CBE keys:
  - `Science8SB31c`: the key opens with "5.1cm" for a part the question doesn't print.
  - `Science6RAJ6b`: a 4-mark, two-part question keyed only "B. Filtration".
  - `Maths10AKP8`: keyed "C. S.A of 2 hemispheres", with no value.
- **D78 (minor).** The generation-time competency sentence and per-set lines are never stored. Generation saves set A's copy over the paper (an overwrite that already existed). So:
  - a reopened paper shows no share;
  - after the first edit the "whole syllabus would give" / "bank cannot reach" clause and the "Set B/C" lines are gone.

  Information is lost, but nothing contradicts the paper.
- **D79 (cosmetic).** A below-target paper states set A's share twice, rounded two ways ("11 of 37 (30%)" and "Set A has 29.7%").
- **D80 (docs, fixed with this audit).** `HANDOVER.md` said "most papers cannot reach 50%". Measured: 46 of 56 whole-syllabus presets meet it.
- **D81 (caveat on 3.5).** The share counts every CBE item by its source. 22 of the 56 served English items that carry assessment-objective marks are knowledge-only (AO1). Maths and Science items carry none, so the share is an upper bound. The report states its rule.
- **D82 (evidence gap on 2.2).** The new widgets are never rendered at 1.3x text: the 1.3x phone tests use fixtures without the new fields. Nit: in `scope_step.dart` the `AvailabilityPanel` doc comment now sits above `_CompetencyLine`.
