## Found by this re-measure (new)

1. **Re-applying leave reuses cancelled cover rows** (`cover.py:478-484`). A second leave on a day with a cancelled one keeps the old leave id. After that, cancelling leaves duties live (`cover.py:520`), and naming a replacement finds 0 periods (`cover.py:565`). 12 "proposed" duties were left after all 3 test leaves were cancelled.
2. **The plan warning's count is a loop variable** (`scheduling.py:335`).
3. **Lessons past the year end are dropped silently** when a working day is added (`routes.py:1428`), and are not restored when the day is removed.
4. **The parent sees no holidays** (403 on `/my-calendar`).
5. **The API docs point to the removed "API keys" page** (#104).
6. **QB-5:** model-written questions are served without reviewer approval (above).
