### Claim 4 — D1/D2/Inter, verified on the deployed bundle

I did not take the source's word for it; I read `main.dart.js` off

## 5. The single highest-value change

**Wire `/api/v1/paper-timing` and `/api/v1/catalog` into the principal console —
one screen, two endpoints that already work and are already principal-gated.**

| Item | Now | After | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.2 Time saved this term, labelled estimated | 0.5 | 0.75 | **+0.63** |
| 5.4 Bank coverage per subject and grade | 0.5 | 0.75 | **+0.63** |
| 6.3 Surfaced to the principal | 0.25 | 0.75 | **+0.83** |
| | | **subtotal** | **+2.08** |

Both endpoints return good, honest payloads today — `/paper-timing` even ships a
`baselineProvenance` string that says the baseline is declared rather than
measured, which is exactly the standard §8.2 asks for. Both refuse a teacher with
a 403. A grep of the entire Flutter client for
`paper-timing|paperTiming|timeSaved|minutesSaved` returns **zero hits**. There is
no backend work, no corpus work and no new API in this; it is one tab beside the
six in `principal_admin_page.dart:18-29`.

**This has been the cheapest win on the board for three audits running and has
not moved once.** It is now also the only remaining item that is pure wiring —
every other route to points needs corpus work or design work.

The close second, and the biggest single-theme move: **give the bank a real
per-question difficulty, Bloom level and CBQ type** (D3). 1.4 0.25 → 0.75 =
**+1.43**, and it converts "parallel sets, tiers, CBQ" from three switches that
truthfully announce they do nothing into a feature. The 00912e7 work made the
product honest about this; it did not make it work, and the honesty is already
banked in 3.5, so there is no further credit to collect without the corpus.

Third: **make `/v1` serve what the builder will print** (D17). 7.3 0.5 → 0.75 =
**+0.42** — low points, but it is the difference between selling a bank and
selling 176 unanswerable questions inside one, and it gates the API product
commercially.

For comparison, serving classes 11 and 12 would be worth 3.1 +0.83, 1.5 +0.71,
3.2 +0.83 = **+2.37**, but that is a new corpus, not a fix.
